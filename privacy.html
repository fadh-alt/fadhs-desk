Privacy policy

dash-io · last updated 23 November 2026

dash-io is a personal dashboard for tasks, classes, projects and files. This page explains what it stores, where, and what control you have.

Without an account

Everything you add stays on your own device, in your browser's storage. Nothing is sent to us.

With an account (sync)

If you sign in, dash-io stores the following so it can be the same on all your devices:

  • Your email address and name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and a Google account ID. We do not receive your Google password or access to your other Google data.
  • A scrambled form of your password if you use email sign-in. Your actual password never leaves your device.
  • What you put in dash-io: tasks, events, timetable, pages, notes, references and the files you upload.

This is stored on Cloudflare's servers in a database that only your signed-in devices can read. It is used only to run dash-io for you.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, dash-io stores your device's push address and the titles and times of your next 7 days of reminders, so they can be sent when the app is closed. Push messages travel through your device maker's push service (Apple, Google or Microsoft) and are encrypted.

Connections you choose

If you connect Spotify, Google Calendar or Outlook, dash-io talks to those services directly from your device. Their sign-in stays on that device; it is not stored in your dash-io account.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • dash-io has no ads and no third-party trackers.

Your control

  • Export: Settings → Export data saves everything as a file.
  • Delete: Account & sync → Password and account → Delete my account removes everything stored online right away. Data on your device stays until you clear it.
  • Sign out at any time to stop syncing a device.
